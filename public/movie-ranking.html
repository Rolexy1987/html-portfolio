The Best Movie According to Roly

My Top 3 Movies of all-times

Titanic

These shots strike precisely the right note; the ship calls from its grave for its story to be told, and if the story is made of showbiz and hype, smoke and mirrors–well, so was the Titanic.

A Quiet Place: Day One

The always-great Lupita Nyong’o plays Sam, a hospice stage cancer patient who agrees to a trip into Manhattan for a show with her support group, led by a bearded Alex Wolff (who also starred in “Pig”)

Pan's Labyrinth

Set in Spain in 1944 during the post-Spanish Civil War period under Franco's regime, the story follows Ofelia, an 11-year-old girl who moves with her pregnant mother, Carmen, to a remote military outpost.
